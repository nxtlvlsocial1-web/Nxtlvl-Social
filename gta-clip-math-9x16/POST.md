## Why it's written this way

- The first line is the CTA keyword (house rule), so it shows before "more"; the hook from the reel follows, then the
  math as a numbered list for people who watch muted. The CTA is not repeated further down.
- Search terms people use (clipping campaign, clipper payouts, pay per view, GTA 6 clips) sit in the hook, the list and
  the keyword line, in plain words.
- The disclaimer keeps the brief's framing: example numbers for one campaign's rules, dated, with no earnings promise.
- No emojis anywhere; hashtags in lowercase.
