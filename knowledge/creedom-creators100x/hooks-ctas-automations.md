## 1. Hook formulas

### 1.1 House rules

Modern story arc (CLAUDE.md rule, verbatim): "Write every video script on the modern story arc (`nxtlvl-social-video`
§ 3a): hook at 70 dropped mid-story, first peak (contrast, contrarian take or shocking stat) by 5-15 s, then peak,
release, peak, release, ending on the CTA."

Skill § 3a (verbatim): "Write every script on the **modern story arc**, not the school arc (conflict, rising action,
climax, falling action, resolution): most viewers scroll away before a slow rise pays off. On a 0–100 attention scale:
1. **Start at 70, not 0 (0–5 s).** The hook drops the viewer into the middle of a story that has already started: no
   setup, no intro, no "today I'll show you".
2. **First peak at 90 (by 5–15 s).** A contrast moment, a contrarian statement or a shocking stat: the first hit of
   dopamine.
3. **Release, then peak again.** Short release beats (context, the how) between peaks, then "boom", the next peak (a
   reveal, a twist, a surprising capability). Peak, release, peak, release, until the video is done. No single climax in
   the middle: stack mini peaks and re-hook as long as you can.
4. **Close on the CTA** (the keyword line, e.g. Comment "STORY" and I'll send it to you) with the value of what they
   get, straight after the last peak.
When presenting a script, label each line with its arc beat (hook 70, peak, release, CTA) so the person can see the
shape."

Origin: the owner sent `Video-40179.mp4` on 2026-10-30 ("Use this formula to make script first" / "And remember to
follow this arc from now").

Skill § 3 (verbatim): "**Hook in the first 1.5 s.** The first frame must already show the subject or the headline — no
slow fade-in from black, no logo-first intro." / "**One message per video.** End on a clear CTA card (follow, link in
bio, shop now) held ≥ 1.5 s."

### 1.2 Owner's hook rules (verbatim)

- Problem first, then solution (Creators 100X, 2026-11-12T15:25:37): "the script and hook should be based on the logic of
  people's problem they face and how we can provide a solution based on it"
- Make it feel like a "whoa" in the first seconds (reel-flop, 2026-11-11T10:08:44): "if we're using the topic of making
  the hook strong, why are we not applying this to ourselves, try something miraculous in the first 6-7 seconds that
  really feels like " whoa what is happening in this reel" so i need that feeling suggest me those ways"
- Name the product's unique point (CHECK, 2026-11-13T03:40:15): "No these dont hooks dont emphasize on the fact that
  there is an app that can rate your video before you post"
- Be accurate (CHECK, 2026-11-13T03:35:01): "But when does instagram tells your reel flopped?"
- Keep unrelated content out (CHECK, 2026-11-13T03:32:05): "Na make the script better and also dont include the gta
  aspect"
- Hooks must not be skippable (prompt reel, 2026-11-15T11:34:29): "pls make the hook more engaging people may skip that"
- The hook's text must look premium (explainer, 2026-11-10T13:24:25): "The starting hook texting,styling and how the
  text appears feels very dull and basic make it feel premium"
- Clarity over cleverness (reel-flop, 2026-11-12T07:23:12): "I feel like the dull intentional video may not be
  understood by many viewers so can we outplay that"
- Exact wording swaps the owner made (write-script, 2026-11-17T14:59:19): "Not one fit -> Not one worked"; "off the
  rack -> off the shelf"; "@writescript need not be spoken out loud".

Techniques we used to meet these (as built):
- Cold open with the hook text already on screen at frame 0 (prompt reel v3: "STOP TYPING THIS" + the prompt on
  screen, STOP stamp at 0.14 s; reel-flop: replay card with a SKIPPED stamp).
- Skip-then-callback intro (reel-flop v9-v13): a fake average reel ("Hey guys, welcome back to my channel!") gets swiped,
  then "This video already lost you."
- Callout of the viewer, product-reveal promise, result first, first-person mystery stat, character low point (see 2.1).
- Hook line delivered fast: a 1.2 s mid-line pause was cut to about 0.3 s (prompt reel v3).
- Picker-wheel or word-by-word kinetic hook text, words appearing only as spoken (explainer).

### 1.3 Formulas from the creator audits

From the 12-creator audit (2026-11-12T15:41:09), verbatim:
- "the hook and the topic drive reach, while the "Comment KEYWORD" line turns that reach into leads."
- Five hook formulas that keep coming back:
  1. "News plus a fix, within a day: "X just changed… here's what to do in 30 seconds.""
  2. "GitHub stars as proof: "A file with 170,000 stars…", "3 repos with 150,000 stars…", usually with "free and open
     source"."
  3. "Secret codes or a numbered list: "5 secret codes for ChatGPT"."
  4. "The best tool for each job: the most-saved post in the sample."
  5. "Disbelief: "This shouldn't be possible", "AI is getting out of hand.""
- "Saves come from lists, codes and prompts written out in the caption ... Shares come from wonder clips and news that
  affects the viewer. Comments come from the keyword CTA and from direct questions."
- Length by format: "Lists and codes: 19–26 s. Repo explainers: 23–41 s. Tutorials: 60–90 s. Build demos: about 2
  minutes."
- "Keep reach posts and lead posts separate. Use the keyword only on resource posts: repos, prompt packs, guides. Wonder
  and news posts should end on a question or "follow", so the keyword doesn't cost them reach."
- "I couldn't find a big Indian page running the keyword-DM model for AI tools. That's an open gap you could take."
