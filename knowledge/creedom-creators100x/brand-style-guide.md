### 1.7 Edit style

Pacing:
- Explainer v4 rebuilt on the Nxtlvl promo grammar, "a new idea every half second to second". fps: 30 (v1-v5) ->
  40 (v6-v8, owner "keep the video in 40 fps") -> 37 final (owner "reduce the fps to 37"). Final 37.5 s, 1388 frames.
- CHECK v6 (owner "it still feels very slow"): voice about 1.12x faster, cuts on the music's half-beat grid (99.4 bpm,
  "BEAT = 60 / 99.384"), 0.3-0.38 s whips and zooms instead of 0.5 s glides, punch-in + glass flash on every key
  word, the score scene split in two shots, light streaks on cuts, overshoot entrances, faster panes, more poses, a riser
  into every cut, deep hits on the hook and CHECK. Scenes: hook 0-3.92, contrast 3.92-6.34, creedom 6.34-9.96, score
  9.96-17.21, fix 17.21-18.72, post 18.72-20.83, cta 20.83-23.54 s.
- The 8 pace fixes the owner approved for CHECK ("Go with all of them"): voice 1.12x, a punchier 120-128 BPM track,
  split score scene, snappier transitions, punch-ins on key words, more in-between motion, busier sound, start in motion.
