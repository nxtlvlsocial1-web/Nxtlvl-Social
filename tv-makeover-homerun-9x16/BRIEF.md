## Intent

A reel for Nxtlvl Social's own page. Pixel-art (8-bit) story: a family on a couch watches a TV
whose home screen is full of ads; they switch to a baseball match, the batter hits a home run, and
as he runs the bases each base explains one step of the TV makeover. The focus stays on the TV
where the player runs. Commentator-style voiceover drives the timing.

## Assets

- assets/audio/voiceover.mp3 — ElevenLabs v4 commentator voiceover supplied by the person (34.19 s).
- assets/fonts/press-start-2p.woff2 — Press Start 2P pixel font (SIL OFL 1.1).

## Customizations

- Sound: match crowd cheering, bat crack, element sounds (remote click, slide, pops), and a
  suitable background music bed.

## Notes

- Never name a launcher app or mention Apple / Apple-style UI in the visuals or text.
- Steps: 1st base = new home screen app (ad rows gone), 2nd = clean rounded tiles + wallpapers,
  3rd = drone screensaver when idle, home = one command removes junk apps (safe list, reversible).
- CTA: comment "TV" for the full guide.
- No Nxtlvl brand kit exists yet (brands/nxtlvl/ missing); the pixel palette in SPEC.md is used instead.
