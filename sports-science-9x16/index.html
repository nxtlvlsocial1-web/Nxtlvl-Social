Does an athlete have to look
a certain way to perform
at their best?
Athletes can have different
body types:
ectomorph, mesomorph or
endomorph.
Different shapes.
Different physical
characteristics.
But body shape alone does
not determine sporting
ability.
Because in sports, what
matters
is not just how your body
looks.
It's what your body can do.
Your strength, speed,
endurance, agility,
mobility and, most
importantly,
your game output.
Unlike bodybuilding,
where physique and muscular
development are central
goals, sports performance is
about delivering results
in your game.
Every sport has different
physical demands.
Every athlete has different
needs.
That's why every athlete
needs to be understood
individually.
Through necessary
assessments,
sport-specific fitness goals
and continuous training,
athletes must keep
developing their physical
capabilities.
Different physiques.
Different sports.
Different requirements.
One focus: better
performance.
