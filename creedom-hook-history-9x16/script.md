## Claims and sources (check before render)

- Trailers first ran after the feature; moved before it by the 1930s; first known trailer 1913 (The Pleasure Seekers).
  mentalfloss.com/article/83734, worldwidewords.org/qa-tra2.html
- First legal paid US TV ad: Bulova, 1 July 1941, WNBT New York, about 10 s (some say 9), cost $9.
  emmys.com "July 1, 1941: The Day Commercial Television Was Born"; guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/first-tv-advert
- "About three seconds" is industry guidance (Meta-attributed advice: nail the hook in the first few seconds), not a platform rule; keep "about".
- The Bulova broadcast frame itself is not used (rights unclear); the TV card is drawn in code.
- Scores in the Creedom card are labelled EXAMPLE.
