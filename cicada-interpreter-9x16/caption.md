## Post description (ready to paste)

```
Comment "CALL" and I'll send you the link.

You don't speak Japanese. Your client just heard you in Japanese.

Cicada is an open-source live interpreter for any call on your computer, and no bot joins the meeting:
1. Live subtitles in your language while the other person speaks
2. Hold one key, answer in English, and they hear you in their language right in the call
3. When the call ends, the notes are written: summary, to-dos, and a full transcript
4. Click a to-do and it jumps back to the exact line it came from

Works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Discord and anything else that plays sound on your computer. Mac, Windows, Linux and Android. Over 3,000 stars on GitHub. Notes and natural voices use an AI service key.

Send this to someone with clients abroad.
```

## Auto-DM for CALL (link and setup only)

```
https://github.com/wquguru/cicada

1. Download the installer for your computer from the Releases page (Mac, Windows, Linux or Android).
2. Open Cicada and give it permission to hear system audio.
3. Join your call. Subtitles show up as soon as someone speaks.
4. To answer in their language, hold the right Option key (Mac) or right Ctrl (Windows) and talk.
5. For live notes and natural voices, add an AI service key in Settings > AI Service.
```
