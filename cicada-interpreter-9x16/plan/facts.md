Not said in the reel (true, but caveats): live notes need an AI service key (Ling, OpenRouter, OpenAI-compatible...);
on Apple silicon Macs speech recognition runs on the device; Windows/Linux/Android need an AI key; interpreting on a Mac
installs the BlackHole 2ch virtual microphone. The caption mentions the key.

Illustrative only: "Haruka Mori", the call UI, the notes window and the transcript lines are our own mockups, not
Cicada's real interface. The Japanese lines: 金曜日までに価格表を送っていただけますか？ ("Could you send the price list by
Friday?") and はい、木曜日に送ります。 ("Yes, I'll send it on Thursday."), voiced with Kokoro jf_alpha / jm_kumo.
