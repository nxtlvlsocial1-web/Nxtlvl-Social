How to use it

  1. Replace everything in [brackets] with your product, price, link and customer stories.
  2. Keep one keyword per post and say it in the video and the first line of the caption.
  3. Send one idea and one link per message. Short DMs get read; long ones get skipped.
  4. Wait about a minute before the first automatic reply so it feels natural.
  5. Check the numbers on the last page every week and rewrite the step where people drop off.

04Answer the doubts

Never argue. Answer with one story, one fact or one easy next step.

"TOO EXPENSIVE"

Totally fair. [Customer] said the same before they joined. [Where they were.] [The result they got.] Here's their story: [link]

"NOT RIGHT NOW"

No rush at all. Want me to send you [free resource] so you can start today, and check back in [2 weeks]?

"WILL IT WORK FOR ME?"

It works best for people who [who it's for]. If that's you, yes. [Customer] started exactly where you are.

"I NEED TO THINK"

Of course. What's the one thing you're unsure about? I'll answer it straight.

10Five rules

  1. Reply fast. The first minutes after a comment are when people are most interested.
  2. One message, one idea, one link.
  3. Answer doubts with stories and proof, never with pressure.
  4. Follow up three times at most, then let it go kindly.
  5. Say no to people your product can't help yet. It's the fastest way to be trusted.
