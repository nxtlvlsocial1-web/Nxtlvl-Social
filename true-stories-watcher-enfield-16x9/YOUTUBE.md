About these stories
The Watcher letters were reported to police in 2014. No one was ever charged, and the writer has never been identified. The house sold in 2019 for about $400,000 less than the family paid.
The Enfield case was investigated by Maurice Grosse and Guy Lyon Playfair of the Society for Psychical Research. Some events were later shown or admitted to be faked, and sceptics doubt the rest. The investigators believed some of it was genuine. What you see in this video is how the people involved described it, not proof either way.
