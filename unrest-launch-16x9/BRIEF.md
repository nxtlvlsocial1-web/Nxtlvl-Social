## Intent

VIDEO 1 for Unrest, a campus social network starting with Delhi University. A 15-second, high-energy, minimalistic,
modern motion graphics intro. Flat vector, kinetic typography, bold geometric shapes, snappy easing, hard cuts synced
to a driving electronic beat. Strictly monochrome: pure #000000 and #FFFFFF, energy from frame inversions on the beat.
Mood: energetic, Gen Z, confident, stark, rhythmic, bold, high-contrast, premium startup launch.
User's ask: "add sfx, motion graphics animation style, smooth transition, premium UI style edit and generate in landscape".

Timeline from the client brief:

- 0:00–0:02 Hook: white dot pulses, expands into a ring; "Is your college life too boring?" slams in word by word,
  "boring" last, oversized, flickering once between solid and outline; one-frame invert flash; drop.
- 0:02–0:05 Discover: white frame; black line icons (feed card, poll bubble, fest ticket, location pin) pop and morph;
  "DISCOVER." solid, then outline.
- 0:05–0:08 Connect: black; white dots joined by lines into a network, collapsing into two speech bubbles meeting;
  Corridor doors slide open; "CONNECT."
- 0:08–0:11 Experience: dots become a crowd of silhouettes; shapes burst out; fest stage; ticket stamps in; frame
  inverts on the burst; "EXPERIENCE."
- 0:11–0:13 Recap: flash cuts alternating black / white: Feed / Corridor / Hangout / Central, slamming with a shake.
- 0:13–0:15 Brand reveal: everything collapses into the white dot, which becomes the Unrest logo on black; tagline types
  in; unrestdu.in · @unrest.du; one confident pulse, hold.

## Assets

  for later videos.

## Customizations

- Music: generated in-house (no third-party samples), minimal techno-house at 120 BPM (brief said ~128; 120 puts every
  section cut from the brief exactly on a beat). Bass drop at 0:02, riser into the 0:13 reveal.
- SFX: clicks, whooshes, pops and impacts synced to every transition, on a separate track. No voiceover.
- Deterministic screen shake on impacts; full-frame inversions via a difference-blend overlay.

## Notes
