# BRIEF — respawn-9x16

- **Client:** Nxtlvl Social (own channel)
- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 54 s. YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok.
- **Look:** blocky 3D (three.js), our series style. Sunset beach city, neon, palms; a cold real-world hospital and a grey rainy street for contrast.
- **Story:** "Respawn" — horror + emotional. A getaway driver keeps waking up on the same hospital steps every time he dies in the city. The city empties, the people left stare at the hospital, his car radio picks up his daughter's voice. Every respawn was a doctor bringing him back after one real crash; he chooses to wake up.
- **Originality:** an original story set in a generic open-world crime-city mood. No game names, characters, logos, map, HUD or lettering from any existing game.
- **Narration:** Kokoro TTS — am_michael (narrator), af_sky pitched up for Lily (radio-filtered on her first line).
- **Music/SFX:** all synthesized in `scripts/make-audio.py`; accents from media-use's bundled Pixabay-licensed library.
- **End card:** "Would you have stayed in the city?" + FOLLOW.
