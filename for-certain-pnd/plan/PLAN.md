# For Certain (PARTYNEXTDOOR): edit plan, first minute

Status: lyrics in (owner, 2026-11-01); line timings estimated from the audio; scene plan below. Waiting on format and pictures.
- The reference is reference/ref_edit.mp4 (960x720, 44.7 s, not in git).
- The song is assets/audio/song.m4a (3:40, not in git). PARTYNEXTDOOR 4, track 9, April 2024.

## 1. What the reference does (frame by frame)

Visual language:
- **Storybook collage.** One recurring cut-out character, an illustrated woman in a red lehenga with a crow as her
  sidekick, walks through painterly worlds:
  - Mughal-miniature paintings
  - dark star-swirl skies
  - a sunset sky
  - parchment, a bank passbook, a luggage tag
- **Literal objects per line.** Every lyric line becomes one object that acts it out:
  - "paanch rupay" gets coins
  - "sheesha" gets a diamond heart
  - "khaali passbook" gets an open empty bankbook
  - "samay" gets a pocket watch
  - "aankhon" gets an eye
- **Type.**
  - Small white bold caps set up the line, then one huge red condensed keyword lands on the beat.
  - Words blur in with motion and go soft as the camera flies past them (depth of field).
  - Last word: "pyaar?" morphs across three scripts (Latin, Urdu, Devanagari).
- **Finish.** Grainy film, soft vignette, warm colour grade, light leaks.

Motion and transitions (full 30 fps, smooth, not stepped):
- **The camera never stops.** A slow push into the scene's focal object, sometimes a slow drift.
- **Portal zooms (its signature).** The next scene lives inside a circle of the current object. The camera dives
  through it in one continuous zoom:
  - pocket-watch face, then the luggage tag
  - the tag's brass eyelet, then an eye
  - the iris, then a kaleidoscope
  - the kaleidoscope cell, then a flower
  - the flower's centre, then the next painting
- **Keyword hits.** The object reacts on the exact frame the red keyword lands: the heart shatters into shards on
  "KAANCH" and the shards keep drifting. Then a push-blur carries into the next scene.
- **Other moves.** Whip blurs between worlds, and soft cross-dissolves through blur for slow lines.
- **End.** The last word holds over the sky, then a credit card.

## 3. The edit, mapped onto the song

Concept: "For Certain" is a man sure he wants her. The reference's grammar fits it well: one recurring character,
and one object per line that acts the line out. The world is PARTYNEXTDOOR's: night, Toronto and Caribbean warmth,
a party with a darker turn (the official video's reading).

Proposed world:
- **Grounds.** Night-blue painterly skies, neon-lit city windows, a party room in warm amber, a sunset.
- **Grade.** Deep blue and amber, with the reference's grain and leaks.
- **Character.** A cut-out of PARTYNEXTDOOR (the owner's photos made into stickers) walks through the scenes, or an
  illustrated silhouette.
- **Type.** White small caps for each line, one big red keyword per line. In PND's palette the red could be a hot
  amber or rose instead; to decide.

Pacing:

| Section | Time | Scenes | How it plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 0 to 20.6 | About 4 scenes (one per 2 bars, 5.2 s) | Slow pushes and soft blur-dissolves, one keyword per line, dreamy. The last 2 bars build with a long push into an object (e.g. a phone screen, a ring box, an eye). |
| Drop | 20.63 | | The portal zoom breaks through into the first full-beat scene, and the keyword slams with a flash and shake on the 808. |
| Sections A and B | 20.6 to 61.9 | About 16 scenes (one per bar, 2.58 s) | Each cut sits on an 808 downbeat; portal zooms alternate with whips. Each triplet roll becomes a three-step stutter push or a reveal (the reference's shatter beat), and the object reacts on the 808. |
| End | 61.9 to 62.5 | | The last keyword holds and the low end falls away, into the logo (dark violet, the song fading across it, as the owner wants). |

Elements to build:
- **The character.** A sticker cut-out that walks or turns per scene.
- **Objects that act the lines out** (chosen once the lyrics are in), each with a reaction on its keyword:
  shatter, spin, open, light up, drop.
- **Portal pairs.** Each object carries a circle (a watch face, ring, lens, phone camera, vinyl label, moon,
  headlight) that the camera dives through into the next scene.
- **Type.** Small white caps plus one red keyword per line, with motion blur in, depth blur out, and the last word
  morphing (e.g. CERTAIN across fonts).
- **Finish.** Grain, vignette, light leaks and a warm/blue grade.
- **Sound.** The song alone, or real CC0 sound effects on the reactions (whoosh on portals, glass on a shatter),
  from Openverse first, then the OpenGameArt packs.

## 6. Scenes: one object per line, portal zooms between them

Colours: night blue and amber. Each scene names an object, its red keyword, its reaction on the keyword and the
circle the camera dives through into the next scene.

Intro, slow, with blur-dissolves and pushes:
1. **First night.** A party seen through a door peephole. Keyword FIRST NIGHT. A crescent moon rises; dive through
   the moon.
2. **Enticin'.** A perfume bottle. Keyword ENTICIN'. Mist sprays on the word; dive through the bottle-cap ring.
3. **Didn't vibe then.** A vinyl record. Keyword THEN. The needle scratches and the record stops dead; dive through
   the label.
4. **But now I want you.** A phone lights up with her name. Keyword NOW (big), then I WANT YOU. Dive through the
   camera lens.

Pre-chorus, the build:
5. **Pure desire.** An eye; neon flames in the iris. The "I-I-I" types in three stutters with the vocal, then
   keyword DESIRE. Dive through the iris (the reference's move).
6. **Heart racin'.** An illustrated heart. It beats on each syllable, an ECG line races across, and AH-OOH-AH
   echoes outward. A long accelerating push into the heart from about 17 s.

Chorus, from the drop; a cut on every 808:
7. **Girlfriend.** The portal bursts on the 808 at 20.63. A ring box snaps open as GIRLFRIEND slams (flash and
   shake). Dive through the ring.
8. **World ends.** The globe turns, and a sun flare blooms on WORLD ENDS. Dive through the sun.
9. **Badman deserves ya.** The PND cut-out, with a crown and halo drawing on. Keyword BADMAN.
10. **Birkins.** Plain designer bags (no brand marks) drop and bounce on the 808. Keyword BIRKINS.
11. **For certain.** The title beat: the cover art as a print, and a stamp CERTAIN slams on the 32.9 roll.
12. **Searchin'.** A magnifying glass sweeps over a search bar. Keyword SEARCH. Dive through the lens.
13. **Woah, woah.** A swirling night sky, like the reference's star swirl. The 35.5 roll becomes a three-step
    stutter push.
14. **And I want you x5.** The phrase stamps five times on the beat, each new frame bigger, as an echo stack, with
    couple polaroids piling up.

Verse 2, the second chance:
15. **Tried on the first night.** A call-back to scene 1: a VHS rewind back to FIRST NIGHT.
16. **Let go of my pride.** A hand reaches out, and PRIDE shatters on the 43.2 roll (the reference's shatter beat).
17. **'42.** A tequila bottle (an unbranded label reading '42) pours on the 45.8 roll. Keyword '42. Dive through the
    glass rim from above.
18. **Not sayin' no tonight.** A neon NO flickers off and TONIGHT lights up.
19. **While you dance.** A disco ball and dancing silhouettes. Keyword HOLD YOU TIGHT; flash on the 53.9 roll.
20. **You know how I want you.** The I WANT YOU type returns.
21. **I need you.** Softer: rain on a window and a candle. Keyword NEED YOU.
22. **The pressure of love.** A pressure gauge's needle climbs into the red. Keyword PRESSURE. At about 62 s the low
    end falls away, then a slow push into the gauge, then the logo (dark violet, the song fading across it).
