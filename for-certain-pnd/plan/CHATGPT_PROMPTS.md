# For Certain: ChatGPT image prompts

29 images for the For Certain edit:
- 7 of the characters
- 9 backgrounds
- 1 eye close-up
- 12 objects

How to use:
1. Make every image in one ChatGPT conversation so the style stays the same.
2. Paste the STYLE block first, then the prompts in order.
3. When a prompt says "attach C1" (or another ID), attach that earlier image so the faces and clothes match.
4. Download each image and name it with its ID (for example `c1_him_profile.png`), then send them all to me.

## STYLE (paste this first, on its own)

```
For this whole conversation, make every image in one consistent style:

Painterly storybook illustration in rich, textured gouache with visible brush strokes and a fine film grain.
Cinematic palette of deep night blue and warm amber, with small touches of rose. Soft rim light, romantic and
moody, slightly dreamlike, like a hand-painted picture book for adults.

Rules for every image:
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, watermarks or signatures anywhere.
- No frames or borders.
- Size: tall portrait (1024x1536).
- I will crop each image to 3:4, so keep everything important inside the middle of the frame, with at least
  10% empty margin at the top and bottom.

When I say CUT-OUT: transparent background (PNG), the subject only, no ground shadow, nothing cut off at the
edges, the whole subject in frame with space around it.

When I say FULL FRAME: a complete scene edge to edge, no transparency.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first image request.
```
