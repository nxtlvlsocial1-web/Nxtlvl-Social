# Creators 100X: "AI Wrote This Reel" (meta), Instagram Reel 9:16, about 28 s

Approved concept 2026-11-23: the reel's own script is the example. Keyword PROMPT -> PDF "10 AI prompts that write your reels".
Style: soft 3D in Creators 100X colours (off-white #FDF4F4, lime #CCFB79 and pink #FE70B3 glows, glossy black #101111,
hot pink #FD0B68 accents). A generic chat window, not a branded ChatGPT screen. No Cree (Creedom's mascot); the 3D
lightning-bolt tile from the logo is the brand element.

## The prompt that wrote this script

1. Role: You're a short-form scriptwriter for Instagram creators.
2. Audience: Creators under 10K followers who post but don't grow.
3. Hook rule: The first line must make them stop scrolling. No greetings.
4. Format: 28 seconds. Problem, mistake, fix, call to action.
5. Tone: Talk like a friend, not a teacher.
Task: Write a reel script showing why a structured prompt beats a lazy one.

## Voiceover: ChatGPT's reply, word for word (owner chose "ChatGPT", 2026-11-23), 83 words

The prompt above was pasted into ChatGPT with a task line asking for a voiceover that admits ChatGPT wrote it and ends on
"Comment PROMPT". Its reply is used unedited; only the ElevenLabs mood tags are added.

1. [flat, monotone, robotic] This reel was written by ChatGPT. [short pause] [warm, amused] And honestly, that's the point.
2. [mocking, flat] If you type, "Write me a reel script," don't expect magic. [beat] You'll get the same generic content everyone else is posting.
3. [matter-of-fact] The mistake? [beat] You're giving AI a task, not direction.
4. [energetic, counting] Give it five things: its role, your audience, your hook rule, your format, and your tone.
5. [lifting, satisfied] Now ChatGPT knows who it's writing for, what to say, and how to say it.
6. [warm, direct] Comment PROMPT and I'll send you the full prompt plus nine more.

## Voice: the AI-to-human switch (owner's pick, 2026-11-23)

One ElevenLabs take, voice Jessica (Eleven v3/v4). Line 1 is read flat; in post it is processed to sound synthetic
(pitch flattened, vocoder-like, a little bitcrush), then a glitch (visual and audio) cuts to Jessica's natural voice on
"And honestly, that's the point." Same voice both sides: the AI voice "becomes human".

## v2 (owner, 2026-11-24): line 2 cut

"And honestly, that's the point." made the point unclear. It is cut from the voice (build/vo_edit.py); a silent beat after
the robot line carries the on-screen text "Sounds robotic?" instead (ElevenLabs no longer available, so no new
recording). The voice is now ChatGPT's script minus that one line; every spoken word is still ChatGPT's.
