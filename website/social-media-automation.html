Social media management and automation for small businesses

On the Social + Automation package, Nxtlvl Social runs your social media for you. We plan the calendar, make the posts and reels, publish them on schedule, answer the common questions in DMs and comments, pass every new enquiry to you, and send a plain-language report on the 1st of every month.

What the automation does

  • Publishes on scheduleYour calendar goes out on time without anyone posting by hand, even on your busiest days.
  • Answers the repeat questionsTimings, price, location and booking questions in DMs and comments get an instant, accurate reply in your words.
  • Routes every enquiryNew enquiries land in one place you already check, so nothing gets lost in a busy inbox.
  • Builds your reportA plain-language report on the 1st of every month: what went out, what worked, what comes next.

What stays human

You approve the content before it goes out. The automation only answers the questions you have given it answers for, and hands anything else straight to you. A real reply from you beats an instant wrong one, so we keep the automated set small and specific.

How social media management works

  1. A 15-minute call. We learn the business and agree the month's plan before anything is made.
  2. Brand kit, shoot and first month. Brand kit in week one, an on-site shoot at your place, and the first month's posts and reels for you to approve.
  3. It runs. Posts publish on your calendar, replies and enquiries are handled, and the report arrives on the 1st.

How is social media management priced?

Social media management is quoted after a free 15-minute call. The package includes everything in Content & Design and Video & Editing, plus the on-site shoot, the scheduled calendar, the automation and the monthly report. No contract, no lock-in.

Questions about social media automation

Will the automation sound like a robot?

No. It only answers the questions you have given it answers for, in your words, and passes everything else to you.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram and Facebook, and we can publish your reels to YouTube Shorts as well.

Do I keep control of my accounts?

Yes. The accounts stay yours. You give us access, and you can remove it at any time.

How much of my time does it take?

A couple of minutes a week to approve drafts. Everything else is run for you.

Can I start smaller?

Yes. Many businesses start with design, add video in month two and hand us the account in month three.
