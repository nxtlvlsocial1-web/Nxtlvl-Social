What's included in social media design

Everything a small business posts in a normal month, designed to look like one business rather than twelve different ones.

  • Feed posts and carouselsInstagram and Facebook posts, plus carousels that walk people through a menu, a service list or a before-and-after.
  • Story setsMatching stories for offers, polls, new arrivals and reminders, sized 9:16 so nothing gets cut off.
  • Posters, menus and price listsPrint-ready and phone-ready versions of the same design, updated when your prices change.
  • Festival and offer creativesDiwali, Holi, Eid, Pongal, Onam, Christmas and your own sale days, planned weeks ahead instead of the night before.
  • A brand kitYour colours, fonts, logo use and post templates, set up in week one so every design looks like you.
  • Captions and hashtagsWritten for each post in plain language your customers use, ready to paste or schedule.

How social media design works

  1. A 15-minute call. You tell us what you sell, who walks in and what is on offer this month. We build your brand kit from it in the first week.
  2. Drafts you approve. First drafts come back the same day or the next. Ask for changes the way you would talk to a person: "make the thali bigger", "use the orange one".
  3. Files ready to post. You get the finished files to post yourself, or move to the Social + Automation package and we publish them on your calendar.

Who it's for

Businesses people walk into and need a steady, good-looking feed for: cafés and restaurants (menus, daily specials, new dishes), salons and spas (service menus, before-and-afters), gyms and studios (class schedules, challenges), boutiques and shops (new arrivals, sale posters), clinics and dentists (timings, health tips, camp announcements), home bakers, coaching centres and hotels.

Questions about social media design

Will my posts look AI-generated?

No. AI gives us the first draft and the variants; a designer picks, fixes and finishes every post. If a post looks generated, it does not ship.

Do I need a logo or brand guide first?

No. We build your brand kit from what you already have, whether that is a logo, a shop sign or a few photos, in your first week.

Can I post the designs myself?

Yes. On the Content & Design package you get the finished files and post them yourself. On the Social + Automation package we publish them for you.

What if I don't like a design?

Tell us in plain language and we redo it. Nothing goes out until you have approved it.

Who owns the designs?

You do. Final files and source files are yours, including the brand kit, and you keep them if you leave.
