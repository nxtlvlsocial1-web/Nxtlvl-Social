FAQ

Creative Studio: common questions

Do you shoot on location?

Yes. We plan and run photo and video shoots on location for products, food, spaces and people. If you prefer, we edit the footage you shoot on your phone.

How fast is the turnaround?

It depends on the format and the monthly plan. Turnaround times are agreed in your scope, and AI-assisted drafting means first versions arrive quickly so there is time for revisions.

Do you use AI-generated images and video?

We use AI for concepts, rough cuts, clean-up and variations. Designers and editors finish every piece, and we never post raw AI output or use AI to misrepresent real products.

Can you follow our existing brand guidelines?

Yes. We work inside your brand guidelines, or create a brand kit if you do not have one yet.

Where can I see your editing work?

Our editing account on Instagram, @nxtlvlledits, shows recent edits. We also share relevant samples on the free call.
