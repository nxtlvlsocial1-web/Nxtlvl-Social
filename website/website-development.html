FAQ

Website Development: common questions

How long does a website take?

A landing page usually takes days and a multi-page website a few weeks, depending on content and features. We share a timeline in your scope before we start.

Is SEO included?

Yes. Every site we build includes on-page SEO, schema markup, a sitemap, fast loading and mobile optimisation. Ongoing SEO and content growth are part of our AI marketing plans.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain, content and code belong to you. We can host and maintain it, or hand it over to your team.

Can you connect the website to WhatsApp and my CRM?

Yes. Forms, WhatsApp buttons and booking tools can feed straight into your inbox, CRM or the automations we build in AI automation.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We can redesign and rebuild an existing site, keeping the pages that already rank and redirecting old addresses so you keep your search traffic.
