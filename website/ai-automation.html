FAQ

AI Automation: common questions

What is AI automation for business?

AI automation uses software and AI models to handle repetitive work such as answering common customer questions, capturing and qualifying leads, sending follow-ups and updating records, so your team only steps in where a person adds value.

Is WhatsApp and Instagram automation allowed?

Yes, when it uses the official tools. We build on Meta's approved WhatsApp Business and Instagram messaging features and follow their policies, including opt-in for broadcasts.

Will customers know they are talking to an automation?

We write replies in your brand voice and make it clear when a message is automated. Customers can reach a person at any point, and complex or sensitive conversations are handed over automatically.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

Not always. Simple setups work with the WhatsApp Business app; larger volumes, multiple team members and advanced flows need the WhatsApp Business Platform. We recommend the right option on the free call.

Which CRM do you use?

We work with what you already have. If you have nothing yet, we set up a simple CRM or a Google Sheet that your team can use from day one.
