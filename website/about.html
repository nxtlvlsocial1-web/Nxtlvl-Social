Growth should not need a big team

Most growing businesses face the same choice: hire an expensive agency, build an in-house team they cannot yet afford, or try to do everything themselves. We started Nxtlvl Social to offer a better option.

We work like a technology company. AI systems handle research, first drafts, repetitive tasks and round-the-clock customer replies. A small, senior team sets the strategy, directs the creative and checks every result. You get a partner that moves fast, costs less than a traditional agency and still feels personal.

What we do

  • Marketing: social media management, campaigns, paid ads, SEO and AEO, for growth on social and beyond it.
  • Automation: WhatsApp and Instagram automation, lead capture, CRM and follow-ups.
  • Creativity: shoots, reel and video editing, graphic design and brand identity, plus websites and landing pages.

How we work

  • AI for speed, people for judgement. Every output is reviewed by a person, and nothing goes live without your approval.
  • Subscriptions, not one-off projects. Growth compounds when the same team learns your business month after month.
  • Clear numbers. We agree on the metrics that matter and report on them every month.
  • You own everything. Files, accounts and systems belong to your business.

What is next

We are building the Nxtlvl growth platform: an AI workspace that turns a brief into ideas, scripts and ready-to-post reels, with our team one message away. Today we deliver that work for clients with AI tools and our own hands; subscribers will be first to use the platform.
