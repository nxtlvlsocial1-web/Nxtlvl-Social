How to shoot footage on your phone for a reel

You do not need a camera crew. Five habits make phone footage easy to edit well:

  1. Hold the phone upright. Reels and Shorts are 9:16, so vertical footage fills the screen without cropping.
  2. Face the light. Stand near a window or the brightest light in the room, with the light on your subject, not behind it.
  3. Record short clips. Several clips of a few seconds each are easier to cut than one long take.
  4. Keep the phone steady. Rest it on a shelf or hold it with both hands and elbows in.
  5. Get close for sound. If someone is talking, keep the phone within arm's length of them in a quiet spot.

Questions about reel editing

Do I need a professional camera?

No. A recent phone is enough. Good light and steady hands matter far more than the camera.

How long should a reel be?

As long as it holds attention and no longer. We cut to the point, which usually means well under a minute.

Can you add music?

We only use music you have the rights to post: royalty-free tracks, or audio you add from Instagram's own library when you publish.

Can you come and shoot for us?

Yes. An on-site shoot is included in the Social + Automation package, within our service area. Ask on the call.

Which platforms do the videos work on?

Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts all use the same 9:16 format, so one edit works on all three.
