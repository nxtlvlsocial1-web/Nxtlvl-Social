FAQ

Questions, answered

Straight answers about how an AI-driven growth company works.

What is Nxtlvl Social?

Nxtlvl Social is an AI-driven growth company in India. We run marketing, business automation and creative production for brands on a monthly subscription. AI systems handle research, first drafts and repetitive work; our team handles strategy, creative direction and quality, and reviews everything before it goes live.

How is an AI-driven growth company different from a traditional marketing agency?

A traditional agency staffs every task by hand, which makes it slow and expensive. We use AI for research, ideas, variations, scheduling and automated replies, so a small senior team delivers more in less time and spends its hours on decisions that move the business. You get the output of a full marketing team without hiring one.

What services are included in a subscription?

Plans are built from four parts: AI marketing (social media management, campaigns, ads, SEO and AEO), AI automation (WhatsApp and Instagram DM flows, lead capture, CRM and follow-ups), the creative studio (shoots, reel and video editing, graphic design) and website development. You choose what you need, and we scope it with you on a free call.

How does the subscription work, and what does it cost?

Each plan has a clear monthly scope and one point of contact. There is no long contract: you can change the scope, pause or stop at the end of any month. Pricing depends on the scope, so we share a written quote after a free strategy call, before any work starts.

Do you work with businesses across India?

Yes. We work remotely with brands across India, from metros to smaller cities, and with international clients. Photo and video shoots are arranged on location when a project needs them.

Will our content look AI-generated?

No. AI speeds up research and drafting, but strategists, designers and editors finish every piece in your brand style. Nothing is published until you approve it.

Who owns the content and accounts?

You do. Final files, source files, ad accounts and any systems we set up belong to your business.

How do we get started?

Book a free call through the contact page. We review your brand and goals, send a scope and plan, and once you approve it we begin onboarding: brand kit, account access and a first-month plan.

Ready to grow with AI on your side?

Book a free 20-minute strategy call. We look at your brand, your channels and your goals, then send a plan you can keep, whether or not you work with us.
