AI Marketing, answered

What is AI marketing?

AI marketing uses artificial intelligence for the parts of marketing that benefit from speed and data: researching trends and competitors, generating content ideas and copy variations, analysing audiences and reporting. At Nxtlvl Social a human strategist directs that work and approves every output.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube for social media; Meta and Google for paid ads; and Google Search plus AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity for SEO and AEO.

Is ad spend included in the plan?

No. Ad spend is paid by you directly to Meta or Google, so you keep full control and ownership of the ad accounts. Planning, creatives and campaign management are part of your plan.

What is AEO, and why does it matter?

Answer engine optimisation (AEO) makes your business easy for AI assistants and Google's AI Overviews to understand and cite. More buyers now ask an assistant before they search, so being the answer it gives is becoming as important as ranking on page one.

How do you measure results?

We agree on the numbers that matter to your business on the first call, such as leads, bookings, sales or reach, and report on them every month along with what we will change next.
