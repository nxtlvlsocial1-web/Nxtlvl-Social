# Creedom "I Wish..." reel: script v1

LENGTH: about 50 s (under 60) · 9:16 · CTA KEYWORD: CREEDOM · HERO: Cree (the Creedom mascot)
Concept (the owner's): an over-dramatic creator is crushed by a flopped reel, lists everything he tried, then makes three
wishes. With each wish Cree is revealed a little more (half-revealed cinematic shots), and on the third he gets the full
hero entrance. Cree never speaks words: he chirps gibberish and a small speech cloud beside him shows what he means.
The creator's lines are real voice, every emotion played big and felt.

Claims: each fix is a real Creedom feature (creedom.ai, Nov 2026): it reviews an uploaded cut second by second before
posting; it reads past reels and what the audience saved; "Comments to DM" automation rules. View counts are examples.
