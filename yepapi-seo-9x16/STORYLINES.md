# Storylines

The client gave the script, so all three follow it beat for beat; they differ in world, device and form.

## A. The question travels (built)

Device: one question carried from need to answer. Form: object-to-object chain.
A magnifier lens x-rays a competitor's page, then grows into the blue world of their keywords and
backlinks; the gap between two bars becomes the prompt box; the question is sent and YepAPI's real
endpoints answer; the answer card gives way to the thumb mark; the key plugs in; price and $5 credit
land; hundreds of lookups fill the screen; the end card asks for the comment.
First 2 s: lemon ground, "SPY ON THEIR SEO" and the lens already over competitor.com.
Look: flat brand colour fields (lemon, thumb blue, sleeve pink, ink), ink outlines, hard offset shadows,
Space Grotesk / Geist Mono. Sound: fast VO, whooshes on world changes, pops on chips, one riser into the mark.

## B. The case file (not built)

The competitor as a spy dossier: stamped folders, redacted rows lifting to show keywords, a
"classified" stamp turning into the YepAPI mark. Bolder, but leans on a cliché.

## C. Type on colour (not built)

Pure giant kinetic type on brand colour fields cutting every half second, no UI at all. Fastest and
loudest; weakest at showing what the product does.
