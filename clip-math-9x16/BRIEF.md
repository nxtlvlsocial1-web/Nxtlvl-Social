# Clip Math: how one campaign's payout rules work

## Intent

A 40 s vertical Instagram Reel, motion graphics only. Neon Miami night: black to deep violet gradient
(#0A0612 to #2A0F4D), violet #8B5CF6 as the main accent, hot pink #FF3D9A and sunset orange #FF8A3D for money,
