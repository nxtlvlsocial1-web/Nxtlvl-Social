## Intent

An original story built on two things people really live with: a door camera that pings at night and a
smart lock. Every 3:12 AM alert shows an empty porch, until he sees each clip starts with his door opening
from the inside. The night he watches live, the lock reports "Front door: locked" from inside. The man who
has been leaving every night is staying, and he is at the bedroom door. Non-graphic: no weapon, no harm shown.

## Customizations

- Narration Kokoro am_michael; the alerts are af_sky with a small-speaker treatment.
- Sets: the bedroom (door now opens onto a hallway with one weak bulb), the house from Legend 3 with a new
  front door, smart lock (thumb-turn + status light) and a porch outside watched by a ceiling camera.
- Porch camera shots: night-vision grade (grey, heavy grain, vignette) plus a HUD canvas (REC/LIVE,
  timestamp, MOTION DETECTED, zoom) drawn per frame over the 3D stage.
- Phone: lock-screen alerts with camera/lock glyphs and a door-camera app screen (clip scrubber, LIVE).
- Generated score and SFX (phone buzz, lock motor + clunk, door creaks, crickets, feed hiss, heartbeat,
  detuned doorbell motif on the end card); bundled Pixabay riser, impacts, glitch, whoosh, pop, click, notification.
- End card comment prompt: "Would you check your camera clips?" + FOLLOW.
