The scariest phone call in America came from inside the house.
I was babysitting two kids. They were asleep upstairs by eight.
At nine, the house phone rang. A man laughed, and hung up.
At ten, it rang again. He whispered,
Have you checked the children?
I called the police. They said, if he calls again, keep him on the line. We'll trace it.
He called again.
Have you checked the children?
I kept him talking for a whole minute.
Then my cell phone buzzed. It was the police.
Get out of the house. Now. The calls are coming from inside the house.
From the phone upstairs.
I looked up the stairs.
Someone was standing at the top. Still holding the phone.
Have you checked the children?
