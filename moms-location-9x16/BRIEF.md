## Intent

Fourth Roblox-style 3D storytime. User asked for "a new trending horror topic with relatable [beats],
[the] reference [style] and a shocking turn", created directly with no checkpoints.
Topic: parent–kid location sharing (a constantly debated, very relatable habit). Hook: at 2:14 a.m.
Mom's dot is in your bedroom. Relatable beats: the location-sharing "deal", texting "are you home?",
the night-shift parent, the closet door that's always open a crack. Turn: Mom lost that phone at home
a week ago, so every "goodnight" all week came from whatever has it, and it's in the closet.

## Customizations

- Narration Kokoro am_michael; Mom's texts in af_heart; Mom's hospital call band-limited like a phone;
  the final "Go to sleep, sweetie" is Mom's voice doubled with a pitched-down copy.
- Phone UI (map with dots and floor plan, messages, call, lock screen) drawn on canvas in 3D; inserts

- Captions one word at a time; "two fourteen" shown as 2:14.
- Generated score and SFX: location pings, message dings (a muffled second ding from the closet when
  the reply lands), an original ringtone, monitor beeps, creak, footsteps; bundled Pixabay riser,
  impacts, glitch, pop, whoosh.
