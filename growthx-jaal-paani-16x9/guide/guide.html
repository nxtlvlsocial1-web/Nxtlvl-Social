THE IDEA

Website = jaal. Traffic = paani.

A beautiful website with no traffic is a beautiful net lying on dry land. And traffic sent to a weak website is ad money poured into the water. You need both, working together.

STEP 1

Find out where your visitors come from

If you said "pata nahi", start here. You need analytics before you spend another rupee on traffic.

If you have Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

1

Open analytics.google.com and choose your website's property.

2

Go to Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition.

3

Set the date range to the last 28 days and look at the Session default channel group column.

4

Read the channels: Paid Social = ads on Meta, Paid Search = Google Ads, Organic Search = Google SEO, Organic Social = your posts and bio link, Direct = typed URL or untracked links, Referral = other websites.

If you're on Shopify

Go to Analytics → Reports and open Sessions by referrer (and Sessions by social referrer) to see which sites and apps send you visitors.

If you have no analytics yet

  • Create a free GA4 property at analytics.google.com and add the tag to your site. On WordPress, the free Site Kit by Google plugin does this; Shopify and Wix have a Google channel or integration.
  • If you run Meta ads, set up the Meta Pixel in Events Manager so you can see which ads bring visitors and leads.
  • Wait 7 days for data, then come back to this step.

Big "Direct" number? That usually means your links aren't tagged, so visits from WhatsApp, your bio link or emails get lumped together. Step 2 fixes this.

STEP 2

Tag every link you share (UTMs)

A UTM is a small tag at the end of a link that tells your analytics exactly where a visitor came from. It's free and takes a minute per link.

The format

yourbrand.com/?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio_link
Where you share itutm_source / utm_medium / utm_campaign
Instagram bio linkinstagram / social / bio_link
Instagram story linkinstagram / social / story_offername
WhatsApp broadcastwhatsapp / message / campaign_name
Meta ads (in the ad's URL parameters)facebook / paid_social / campaign_name
Google Business Profile website buttongoogle / organic / gbp
Email newsletternewsletter / email / issue_name

How to build them

  • Use Google's free Campaign URL Builder (search "GA4 campaign URL builder").
  • Keep everything lowercase and use the same names every time, or your reports will split.
  • Paste the long link into a short-link tool if it looks messy in your bio.

After a week, check Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition again and switch the column to Session source / medium. You'll now see every link you tagged, by name.

STEP 3

Fix the jaal so it holds customers

Open your website on your phone, the way most visitors see it, and tick every point you can.

💰 Rate dikhaya: price and offer

  • Price, or "starting from ₹…", is visible on the main product or service page.
  • What's included is clear in 3–5 bullet points.
  • Any offer has a clear deadline or reason to act now.

⭐ Bharosa diya: trust

  • At least 3 real reviews or testimonials, with names and photos where possible.
  • Real photos of your product, team or work (not only stock images).
  • Address, phone or WhatsApp number, and return or refund policy are easy to find.
  • Ratings, client logos, media mentions or certifications, if you have them.

📞 Button diya: one clear next step

  • One main button above the fold: Buy now, Book a call or WhatsApp us.
  • The same button repeats after every section, so visitors never have to scroll back.
  • Forms ask for 3 fields or fewer (name, phone, need).

⚡ Basics

  • The page loads in under 3 seconds on mobile data (test it at pagespeed.web.dev).
  • Text is readable and buttons are easy to tap on a phone.
  • Each ad or link goes to the page that matches it, not just the homepage.

STEP 4

Pick your paani and run a 30-day plan

If you have…Start with
Budget, and you need leads nowAds. One Meta campaign to your best page, with a small daily budget you can sustain for 2 weeks.
Time, and customers search for what you sellGoogle. Complete your Google Business Profile and write 1 helpful page a week answering real customer questions.
Little budget, but you can create contentSocial media. 3 reels a week, with a tagged link in your bio and stories.

Your 30 days

1

Week 1: measure. Analytics set up (Step 1), all your links tagged (Step 2). Note your current visitors and enquiries.

2

Week 2: fix the net. Work through the Step 3 checklist, starting with price, reviews and the main button.

3

Week 3: add water. Start one traffic source from the table above and keep it running every day.

4

Week 4: review. Compare visitors and enquiries by source. Keep what brings customers, cut what doesn't, then add the next source.

The number to watch: enquiries or sales ÷ visitors, by source. Traffic alone is just water; this number tells you whether the net is catching anything.
