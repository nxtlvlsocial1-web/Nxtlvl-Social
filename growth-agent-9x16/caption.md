## Why it's written this way (SEO)

- Instagram search reads the caption, the alt text and the words on screen. The key phrases (Instagram growth, Claude
  AI, AI agent, grow your page, reels, content creator) are in the first line, the list and the alt text, in plain
  words people search for.
- The first line is the hook and the proof, and it fits before "more" on most phones.
- One message, one CTA: the "AGENT" comment keyword. It only works if an auto-DM for "AGENT" is set up (for example
  in your Auto-DM tool) before the Reel goes live.
- The list repeats the video's five moves, so people who watch muted get the value from the caption too.
