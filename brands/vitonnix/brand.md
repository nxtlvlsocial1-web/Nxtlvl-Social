## What they sell
SubliSpray sublingual vitamin sprays (vitamin D, B12, biotin, melatonin, iron, multivitamins for kids and teens,
pregnancy, omega). Founded by three doctors; developed in the UK, made in India. Sold in India via Tata 1mg,
Flipkart and Amazon.in. Boots UK range announced for 2026.

## Logo
- Blue "Vitonnix" oval with "Future of Vitamins" tagline, seen on pack. No vector file yet; use the packshot.

## Tone
Warm, plain, confident. Convenience and format first (spray, not swallow). Doctor-founded. No absorption
multipliers, no disease claims; only authorised per-nutrient wording if efficacy is mentioned at all.

## Compliance notes
- Absorption multipliers: the website says "5X more effective", the Biotin creatives say "9X better absorption".
  The Biotin reel (vitonnix-biotin-9x16) uses "9X" because the client supplied it as a stat. The two figures must be
  reconciled and backed by the client's substantiation before paid use; ASCI (India) and ASA (UK) can ask for it.
- UK-facing content: no melatonin (prescription-only in the UK); ASA/CAP health-claim rules.
- India: ASCI and FSSAI rules; influencer disclosure.

## Spelling
Use "SubliSpray" (the client's own copy also uses "Sublispray"; pick one with them).
