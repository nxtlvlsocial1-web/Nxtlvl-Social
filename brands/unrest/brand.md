# Unrest brand kit

Unrest is a campus social network for Delhi University students.

- Tagline: "Your Campus. Your Network."
- Web: unrestdu.in · Instagram: @unrest.du
- Story: Discover (what's happening) → Connect (who can I meet) → Experience (what can I join).
- Product names: Feed, Corridor, Hangout, Central. Concepts outside the app: Unrest House, Unrest Card, Unrest+.

## Colour

Strictly monochrome. No other colours, no greys, no gradients.

| Token | Hex       | Use                          |
| ----- | --------- | ---------------------------- |
| black | `#000000` | Primary background           |
| white | `#FFFFFF` | Type, logo, line work, flashes |

Energy comes from contrast: hard cuts and full-frame inversions (black on white ↔ white on black).

## Logo

- `logo-light.svg`: white wordmark plus tagline, for black backgrounds (primary use).
- `logo.svg`: black version for white backgrounds.
- `wordmark-light.svg`: white wordmark only, no tagline.
- The wordmark is a Times-style serif: "Un" upright, "rest" italic. These SVGs were rebuilt from the
  client's 1179×662 JPG using Liberation Serif outlines fitted letter by letter. Swap in the client's
  original vector file if they send one.
- Keep it centred on black, with clear space of at least the height of the "U" on every side.

## Type

| Role                    | Font                              | File                                          |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Motion / display        | Space Grotesk 700 (500 for small) | `fonts/space-grotesk-latin-{700,500}-normal.woff2` |
| Logo tagline, serif notes | Liberation Serif (Times-compatible) | `fonts/LiberationSerif-{Regular,Italic}.ttf` |

Both are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Display type is huge with tight tracking (about −0.04em). Key words can
switch to outline (stroke only) for emphasis.

## Tone

Energetic, Gen Z, confident, stark, rhythmic, bold, premium startup launch.

## Do / don't

- Do: flat vector, kinetic type, bold geometric shapes, snappy easing, hard cuts on the beat, frame inversions,
  subtle shake on impacts.
- Don't: colour, grey tints, gradients, stock photos, cluttered UI screenshots, college clichés
  (graduation caps, books), slow fades.
