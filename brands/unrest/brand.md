# Unrest (client)

- **What it is:** a verified social network only for Delhi University students. Every user is a verified DU student, so
  every person you meet on it is a real one.
- **Who it's for:** DU students across all colleges, first years to final years.
- **What it does:** on-ground events, live matching through Corridor, flatmates, part-time work, internships, and (new)
  the course feed and library rooms.
- **The one feeling:** you're not doing DU alone. Everyone here is a DU student, so every scroll is someone you could
  actually meet.
- **Tagline:** "Your Campus. Your Network."
- **CTA:** Comment "UNREST" and get the link in DM.

## Look
- **Logo:** white serif wordmark "Unrest" ("rest" in italic) on black; animated version in
  unrest-promo-9x16/assets/video/unrest_logo.mp4 (owner's file, 1440x1440, 5 s blur-in). Keep it white on black.
- **Colours (proposed, owner to confirm):** Black #0A0A0B, Ink card #141416, White #FFFFFF, Mute #8A8A90,
  accent Electric lime #CCFF4D (the one accent: buttons, badges, highlights).
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (OFL, close to the wordmark) for headlines, italic for the second half of a line;
  Satoshi (Fontshare ITF FFL) for interface text; JetBrains Mono for small labels.
- **Music:** instrumental, cut to the beat, with sound effects on every tap and pop (owner, 2026-11-24).
