## Type
Bold, wide geometric caps. In video we use Inter Display (Black / Bold / SemiBold); wide letter-spacing for small caps labels like the logo wordmark.

## Look
Black stage, neon purple glow, thin rotating rings echoing the badge, upward arrow motif (growth).
