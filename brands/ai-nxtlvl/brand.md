# AI.NXTLVL

The ai.nxtlvl page (the AI explainer videos). Its logo is the **nxtlvl wordmark**: lowercase "nxtlvl" in white with a
violet four-point star above the last letter. Not to be confused with NXTLVLSOCIAL.AI, whose logo is the comet-star
mark in `brands/nxtlvl/`.

## Logo

- `logo.png`: the original the owner shared, 1080x1080, the wordmark on its black and violet gradient.
- `logo-wordmark.png`: the wordmark cut out on transparency (790x244), white letters, violet star. Use on dark.
- `logo-wordmark-dark.png`: the same with near-black letters, for light backgrounds.
- In video top bars and labels the handle is written `AI.NXTLVL` in JetBrains Mono.

## Colours

Sampled from `logo.png`.

| Name     | Hex       | Use                                        |
| -------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Night    | `#07050f` | main background (the logo's black centre)  |
| Deep     | `#10022c` | cards, panels                              |
| Violet   | `#7f1bf8` | gradient glow, top-left corner             |
| Electric | `#6008f2` | gradient glow, bottom-right corner         |
| Star     | `#7a2ffb` | the star, accents, numbers                 |
| Lavender | `#b687f9` | labels, accent words                       |
| White    | `#ffffff` | the letters, headlines                     |

Backgrounds follow the logo: near-black with soft violet glows in opposite corners.

## Type

- Headlines: Montserrat or Manrope 800, tight tracking.
- Accent words: Instrument Serif Italic.
- Labels, code, the handle: JetBrains Mono.

## Tone

Plain, confident, practical. No emojis in post descriptions, captions or hashtags (house rule).
