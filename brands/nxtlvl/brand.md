# Nxtlvl Social (NXTLVLSOCIAL.AI)

Draft kit, built from the logo the owner shared (`logo.jpg`). Colours are sampled from it; the fonts are our pick
until the owner names their own.

## Logo

- `logo.jpg`: the original, 1080x1080, the mark on its purple gradient.
- `logo-mark.svg`: the mark redrawn as a vector (a four-point star with three comet trails), for print and PDFs.
  Use it on the dark backgrounds below, never on white.
- Wordmark: `NXTLVLSOCIAL.AI` in JetBrains Mono, wide letter-spacing, next to the mark.

## Colours

| Name     | Hex       | Use                                             |
| -------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Night    | `#07050f` | main background                                 |
| Deep     | `#140a2e` | cards, panels, code blocks                      |
| Violet   | `#6516c7` | the gradient's glow (top-left)                  |
| Electric | `#4b06c1` | the gradient's second glow (bottom-right)       |
| Orchid   | `#a75bfc` | accents, links, highlights                      |
| Lavender | `#b687f9` | labels, secondary accents                       |
| White    | `#ffffff` | headlines, the star                             |
| Mist     | `#cfc8dd` | body text on dark                               |

Backgrounds are the logo's look: near-black with soft violet glows in opposite corners.

## Type

- Headlines: Montserrat 800, tight tracking.
- Accent words: Instrument Serif Italic (one or two words per headline, in lavender).
- Labels, code, the wordmark: JetBrains Mono.

## Tone

Plain, confident, practical. No emojis in anything we write for posts, captions or hashtags (house rule).
