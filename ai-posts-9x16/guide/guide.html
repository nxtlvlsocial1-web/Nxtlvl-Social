STEP 1Record a 2-minute voice memo

Hit record and talk without stopping. Do not script it. If you are stuck, pick one starter:

  • What I do every morning and why
  • A mistake that cost me money or time
  • The advice I would give myself a year ago
  • Something everyone in my niche gets wrong
  • A tool or habit I cannot live without
  • The last thing that annoyed me (a rant)
  • How I got my first client or follower milestone
  • A question people keep asking me
  • What I stopped doing this year
  • A small win from this week

TIP Record three memos on different topics and you have a 90-post bank.

STEP 2The 30-ideas prompt

Copy your transcript, then paste this prompt into ChatGPT or Claude. Fill in the parts in blue.

You are my content strategist. Below is a transcript of me talking for two minutes. I post on Instagram about [your niche] for [who your audience is]. From this transcript only, give me 30 post ideas. For each idea: 1. A working title (max 8 words) 2. The format: carousel, reel or single image 3. The exact moment or sentence from my transcript it comes from Rules: use my words, my stories and my opinions. Do not invent facts, numbers or experiences I did not mention. Mix: 10 stories, 10 tips, 5 opinions, 5 questions for my audience. Transcript: [paste transcript here]

STEP 3Hook, caption and slides

Run this for each idea (or five at a time). Keep the ones that sound like you, rewrite the rest.

Take idea #[number] from your list. Write: 1. Three hook options for the first slide or the first 2 seconds (max 10 words each, no clickbait, no questions people can answer "no" to) 2. A 7-slide carousel: slide 1 = hook, slides 2-6 = one point each in my words, slide 7 = a takeaway plus "save this" 3. A caption: first line = the hook, then 3 short paragraphs, then one question for comments. Plain text, no emojis. 4. Five hashtags in lowercase that fit the post. Sound like the transcript: same slang, same tone, short sentences.

STEP 4Schedule all 30 for free

Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com or the app) has a free planner for Instagram and Facebook.

  1. Open Planner and pick a time slot (one post a day is plenty).
  2. Create post, add your slides and paste the caption.
  3. Choose Schedule, set the date and time, repeat for the month.
  4. Check your best posting times in Insights and move posts there.

CHECKBefore anything goes live

  • Read every caption out loud. If you would not say it, rewrite it.
  • Delete any fact, number or claim you did not actually say.
  • Swap generic words for your own phrases from the memo.
  • Keep one clear message per post.
  • Reply to comments yourself. That part cannot be scheduled.
