Can an ₹85,000 device measure your peace of mind?

Deepinder Goyal's Temple tracks your brain's blood flow and how your body responds to stress, sleep and exercise. But there is a wellness tracker that costs nothing, needs no subscription and takes none of your time: the people around you.

As a doctor, I can tell you that the people in our environment affect our peace of mind and mental health the most. Sometimes the greatest upgrade to your mental health is not a device. It's the company you keep.
