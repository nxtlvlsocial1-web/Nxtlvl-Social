Before you start

  1. Go to notebooklm.google.com and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Gather everything: lecture PDFs, slides, Google Docs, and photos of handwritten notes (scan them to PDF with your phone first).
  3. One notebook per exam. Give it the exam's name, e.g. Biology final.
  4. The free plan has daily limits on audio, flashcards and quizzes. Check the current limits in the app.

01Upload your notes

Click Add sources and drop in every file. NotebookLM only answers from what you upload, and every answer links back to the page it came from.

GOOD SOURCES

Lecture slides, your notes, the textbook chapters on the syllabus, past papers.

CHECK IT

Ask: "Summarise what my sources cover, chapter by chapter." Missing a chapter? Add it.

02Turn them into a podcast

In the Studio panel, open Audio Overview, click Customize, paste a focus prompt, then generate. Two AI hosts talk through your notes. Listen on the bus, at the gym, before bed.

FOCUS PROMPT

Explain my notes for an exam on [date]. Start with the big ideas, then the definitions and formulas I must memorise. Spend extra time on [chapter].

SHORTER VERSION

Pick the Brief format for a 5-minute recap the night before.

03Flashcards + a practice test

In Studio, create Flashcards and a Quiz. Choose the number of cards and the difficulty, then take the quiz without looking at your notes.

FLASHCARDS PROMPT

Make flashcards for every definition, formula and date in [chapters]. One fact per card.

QUIZ PROMPT

Write a hard practice test in the style of my past papers. Mix multiple choice and short answers.

04Fix your weak spots

After the quiz, ask the chat about everything you got wrong, then make a new quiz on only those topics. Repeat until your score climbs.

ASK THE CHAT

I got these questions wrong: [paste them]. Explain each one simply, show where it is in my notes, and tell me the trick to remember it.

RETAKE

Make a new 15-question quiz only on [chapter 7], harder than the last one.

06Prompts worth stealing

EXPLAIN IT

Explain [topic] like I am 12, then like a professor, then give me one example that would appear in an exam.

MEMORY HOOKS

Turn the 10 hardest facts in my notes into short memory tricks or rhymes.

PREDICT THE EXAM

Based on my notes and past papers, which 5 questions are most likely to come up? Answer each in 3 lines.

07Rules

  1. Click the citations: every answer shows the page it came from. Check anything that matters.
  2. Only upload your own notes and course material, nothing private or confidential.
  3. The AI helps you study. You still sit the exam.
