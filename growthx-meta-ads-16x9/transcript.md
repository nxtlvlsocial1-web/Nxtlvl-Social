## Full script (voiceover)

Agar apne D2C brand abhi start kiya hai, to yeh Meta Ad Strategy apko direct customer lake degi, karna kuch nahi hai. Bss ek campaign bnao, Sales Conversion objective ke, aur usmei 3 ad set add karo: Broad Audience, Interest Targeting, aur lookalike audience, jo apki website visit ya video views pe based ho, aur jisne apke Instagram pe engage kiya hai, usko lookalike mei daldo. Aur creative, jo sabse important hai sales ko scale karne ke liye. Full detailed PDF chahiye? Comment karo META, aur PDF seedha aapke DM mein.
