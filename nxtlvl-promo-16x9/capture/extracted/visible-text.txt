Questions
The things people ask before saying yes
Not answered here? Message us on WhatsApp and a person replies.
Is $5 really the whole price?
It is the starting price for the Content & Design plan, billed monthly. Higher volumes and extra services move you up a plan — we tell you the number before anything starts, and it does not change mid-month.
What if I don't like what you make?
Tell us in plain language and we redo it. Nothing goes out on your account until you've approved it.
Do I have to sign a contract?
No. It's month to month. Stop at the end of any month and nothing carries over — no notice period, no exit fee.
Who owns the files?
You do. Final files and source files are yours, including the brand kit, and you keep them if you leave.
Will my feed look AI-generated?
That's the thing we work hardest to avoid. AI gives us the first draft and the variants; a designer picks, fixes and finishes. If a post looks generated, it doesn't ship.
Where do you do on-site shoots?
Shoots are included in the $49 plan within our service area, and we travel further for a small charge. Ask on the call and we will tell you straight away.
I have no footage and no photos. Can you still start?
Yes. Most businesses start with nothing. We can design your first month entirely without a shoot, then book one once you've seen it working.
Do you post for me, or do I post?
Either. On the design and video plans we hand you the files and you post. On the $49 plan the automation publishes on your calendar, so you never think about it.
Do you handle ads too?
We build the creatives — multiple hooks and thumbnails per campaign, made for testing. Ad spend and account management are quoted separately.
What exactly does the automation do?
Four things. It publishes your calendar on schedule so nothing is posted by hand. It answers the repeat questions in DMs and comments — timings, price, location, booking. It drops every new enquiry into your WhatsApp or a spreadsheet so nothing gets lost in a busy inbox. And it builds your monthly report for you. You approve the content; the rest runs without anyone watching it.
Will the automation sound like a robot to my customers?
It only answers the questions you have given it answers for, in your words, and it hands anything else straight to you. A real reply from you beats an instant wrong one, so we keep the automated set small and specific.
How do I send you things?
WhatsApp. Photos, voice notes, "post this today" — whatever is easiest. You don't need to learn a tool.
