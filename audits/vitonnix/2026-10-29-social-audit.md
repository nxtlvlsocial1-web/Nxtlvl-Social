# Vitonnix: social and web audit

Date: 29 Oct 2026. Subject: vitonnix.com (Vitonnix Ltd, SubliSpray sublingual supplements). Prepared for Nxtlvl Social.

Method: desk audit. The cloud session's network policy blocked vitonnix.com and vitonnix.in, so no page was loaded directly. Facts come from indexed copies of their pages, October 2026 funding coverage, retailer listings and social-profile snapshots. Follower counts are snapshots of unknown date; re-check live before any goes in a deck.

## Who they are

- Product: SubliSpray sublingual nutritional sprays, 12 to 20 ml. About 27 to 28 SKUs (vitamin D, B12, biotin, melatonin, iron, glutathione, veg omega, energy, pregnancy, kids and teen multivitamins). Pet line Vetonnix (5 SKUs on Supertails and Tata 1mg).
- Founders: three practising doctors. Dr Prashant Jindal (refractive eye surgeon, UK), Dr Deepti Jindal (dermatologist), Dr Rajesh Handa (oral surgeon). Chairman Darshan Lal Jindal.
- Footprint: developed in the UK, formulated and made in India, spray systems engineered in Germany. Offices in Mumbai and Chandigarh; UK company registered in Solihull, operations from London and Birmingham.
- Distribution: exclusive India partnership with Tata 1mg since January 2024 (co-branded listings), plus Flipkart and Amazon.in. Boots UK range of about ten products announced April 2026 "from Summer 2026"; no live Boots listing found.
- Money: first institutional seed round led by Inflection Point Ventures, announced mid-October 2026, size undisclosed. Investors include Riteish Deshmukh, Boman Irani, Jaspinder Narula, Madhu Mantena. Stated use: senior hires, a consumer-growth engine, distribution, UK/UAE/US entry.
- Price (India): Rs 499 list on vitonnix.in, Rs 360 to 400 discounted on Flipkart.

## Web and commerce

- vitonnix.com: corporate and catalogue. Pages: home, about-us, leadership, contact-us, csr, collabs, ukcollabs, indpartners, our-products, /product/ pages, vitamin D blog post. URLs carry `?id=` parameters.
- vitonnix.in: Shopify-pattern India D2C store, Rs 499 pricing, on-site reviews.
- vitonnix.co.uk: UK site, content not seen.
- Retail: Tata 1mg, Flipkart, Amazon.in, Supertails (Vetonnix). Retail listings outrank brand pages for several product searches.
- Brand name spelled three ways in their own copy: SubliSpray, Sublispray, "Nutritional Sublispray".

## Claims and compliance

- "5X more effective" has no cited study; a systematic review of vitamin D3 buccal spray trials found only four eligible RCTs.
- "Three clinical studies" and "millions of bottles" are company statements; ask for the studies.
- UK: ASA and CAP Code; only authorised GB NHC register claims. India: ASCI and FSSAI; influencer disclosure.
- Melatonin is prescription-only in the UK; keep it out of UK-facing content.
- Our content leads with convenience, format, taste and the doctor story; efficacy language limited to authorised per-nutrient claims.

## What is working

- Visual product: a spray under the tongue is a two-second demo.
- Three doctors across eye, skin and oral surgery for on-camera authority.
- Retail CTAs: "Available at Tata 1mg", "Coming to Boots".
- Family range (kids, teen, pregnancy, pets) opens parent and pet-owner audiences.
- CSR drives give non-product brand content.
- Existing Collabs and UKcollabs pages show creator appetite.

## Gaps

- Handle hygiene across Instagram, X, Facebook and two YouTube channels.
- No short-form video found ahead of a UK pharmacy launch.
- Funding news not converted into consumer-facing content.
- Three storefronts with no clear home or shared social link set.
- Reviews concentrated on the brand's own site.
- Category-generic copy ("The Future of Vitamins").

## Content pillars

1. Doctor explains: 9:16, 20 to 45 s captioned talking head (talking-head-recut, embedded-captions).
2. The two-second demo: 9:16, 7 to 12 s loopable, one SKU per clip, retail CTA (motion-graphics, product-launch-video).
3. How sublingual works: 9:16 and 1:1, 30 s faceless, compliant language (faceless-explainer).
4. Parents and pets: 9:16, 15 to 30 s creator-shot UGC (ugc, embedded-captions).
5. UK launch countdown: 9:16 and 16:9 series around the Boots arrival (general-video).
6. Investor and retail story: 16:9 and 1:1 for LinkedIn (slideshow, milestone-reveal).

## First 30 days

1. Unify one handle, bio and link set; settle SubliSpray spelling; separate India and UK link destinations.
2. Brand kit into `brands/vitonnix/`.
3. One half-day founder shoot, target 24 clips plus B-roll.
4. Twelve demo spots: six hero SKUs, two use moments each, 9:16 with 4:5 cutdowns.
5. Creator seeding: ten India parents and pet owners via their Collabs page, with disclosure rules and caption templates.
6. Baseline and weekly reporting against the "consumer-growth engine" line in their funding release.
