Features

Everything you need to split costs fairly

From weekend getaways to monthly rent, Tally handles the numbers so you can focus on the fun.

Create groups

Set up a group for your trip, house, or team in seconds and invite friends with a link.

Flexible splits

Split equally, by exact amounts, percentages, or shares. Tally does the math either way.

Scan receipts

Snap a photo of any receipt and Tally pulls out the items so everyone can claim what they had.

Simplify debts

Our smart algorithm minimizes the number of payments needed, so settling up takes fewer transfers.

Multi-currency

Traveling abroad? Add expenses in any of 150+ currencies with automatic exchange rates.

Settle instantly

Pay friends back right in the app with Venmo, PayPal, or bank transfer. One tap and you're even.
