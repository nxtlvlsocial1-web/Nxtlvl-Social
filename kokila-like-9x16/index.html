Not everyone
will like you
No matter how good
you are or
how much good
you do
there will always
be people who won't
like you.
They'll complain
about you
talk about you and
find something wrong
in everything you do.
And honestly
that can hurt.
But their opinion
is not a reflection
of who you are.
Sometimes it's a reflection
of who they are
their insecurities
their mindset and what
they carry inside.
So don't let
someone else's negativity
become your reality.
Keep doing good
keep growing
and let them talk.
Because you don't need
everyone to like
you.
You just need
to like yourself.
