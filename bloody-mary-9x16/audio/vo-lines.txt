Never say her name three times in the dark.
At my sleepover, my friends dared me to do Bloody Mary.
Lights off. One candle. Just me and the mirror.
The rule is simple. Say her name three times, and she appears behind you.
Bloody Mary.
Bloody Mary.
I stopped. I was not saying it a third time.
My friends were giggling outside the door.
Then my reflection said it for me.
Bloody Mary.
My lips never moved. But in the mirror, they did.
I ran out. My friends looked at me like I was crazy.
They said, we heard two of you in there.
I looked back into the bathroom.
My reflection was still in the mirror. Without me.
And it was waving.
