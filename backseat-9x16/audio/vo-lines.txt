If a car flashes its high beams at you at night, don't get mad. It might be saving your life.
I was driving home late, on an empty country road.
A pickup truck pulled out behind me. And it would not stop following.
Every few minutes, it flashed its high beams. Right into my car.
I sped up. It sped up.
By the time I pulled into my driveway, I was shaking.
The truck pulled in right behind me.
The driver jumped out and ran at my car, yelling.
Get out of the car! There's someone in your back seat!
Every time he flashed his lights, the man in my back seat had been sitting up.
And every time, he ducked back down.
I looked in the mirror.
He wasn't ducking anymore.
