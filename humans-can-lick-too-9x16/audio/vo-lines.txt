My dog licked my hand all night. At least, I thought it was my dog.
My parents were gone for the night. It was just me and Max.
Max always sleeps under my bed.
At midnight, my phone buzzed. A man had escaped from the prison nearby.
I locked every door. I turned off every light.
Then I heard it. Drip. Drip. Drip.
I was too scared to get up. So I reached my hand under the bed.
And Max licked it. Like he always does.
Every time the dripping woke me up, I reached down, and he licked my hand.
In the morning, the dripping was coming from the bathroom.
Max was locked in the shower. Shaking.
He'd been in there all night.
And written on the foggy mirror...
Humans can lick too.
So whatever was under my bed...
is still under my bed.
